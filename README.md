### 🚀 What I've built (since May 2026, ~100 hours with AI)
- 💼 **First client website: The Garrett Company**, a 3rd-generation real estate brokerage in Franklin, TN (est. 1947). It has cinematic Nashville drone video, a self-drawing legacy timeline, a gallery of 21 recent sales, and a typo-tolerant Middle Tennessee search covering 283 ZIPs. → **[Live preview](https://garrett-company-site.milesgurule.workers.dev/)**
- 🕳️ **[Forge Labs studio site](https://forgelabs-agency-site.milesgurule.workers.dev/)**: a WebGL spark world that forges the wordmark and re-forges it into your company name, then a **black hole ray-traced live in your browser**: every pixel follows a beam of light through curved space, and as you scroll the site's sparks spiral in and the camera dives into the shadow. Tuned to hold ~60 fps on a Retina laptop.
- 🧪 **Forge Labs Science Lab**: my AI agent team visualized as scientists in a 3D research facility. → **[Live demo](https://milesgurule-png.github.io/forge-showcase/lab/)**
- ⚛️ **Vault Atom**: about 500 research notes drawn as one connected 3D atom, with 4,000+ cited links. → **[Live demo](https://milesgurule-png.github.io/forge-showcase/atom.html)**
- 🤖 **Forge Labs agent team**: 9 specialized AI research agents (historian, psychologist, quant, physicist, venture architect) run by a director agent. Together they've written 1,000+ evidence-rated notes.
- 🧠 **[Forge AI](https://github.com/milesgurule-png/forge-ai)**: my own open-source assistant that runs on my laptop and answers from my notes with citations (local RAG, Apache-2.0).
- ⚛️ **[Vault Atom for Obsidian](https://github.com/milesgurule-png/obsidian-vault-atom)**: an open-source plugin that shows any Obsidian vault as a 3D atom (MIT).
- 🎯 **[Triage Scorecard](https://github.com/milesgurule-png/triage-scorecard)**: scores any vulnerability-prioritization method against real CISA KEV and EPSS data, with no future data (MIT). Its daily **KEV Watch** shows how many newly exploited CVEs EPSS missed the day before (on Oct 18, 2026: 6 of 9).
